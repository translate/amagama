# amaGama Authors

These are people and organisations that have contributed to amaGama.

More detailed contribution statistics can be found on GitHub's
[contributors page](https://github.com/translate/amagama/graphs/contributors).

## Historical perspective

amaGama was created in 2010 by Alaa Abd el Fattah for Translate.org.za
(the Zuza Software Foundation), as a port of the Translate Toolkit's
tmserver to Flask. Friedel Wolff, Dwayne Bailey and Leandro Regueiro have
been its long-term maintainers since.
